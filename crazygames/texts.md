# CrazyGames: texts for the submission form

Copy each block whole. The game itself speaks English, Russian, Romanian,
Spanish, Portuguese and German, and starts in the player's own language.

**Title**

```
Ice Dash
```

**Short description** (one line, for cards and search)

```
One tap sends the penguin the other way. Weave down the ice, catch fish, dodge the trees!
```

**Description**

```
Slide down an endless ice chute on your belly. You are always drifting to one side, and one tap sends you the other way. That is the whole control: easy to learn, hard to master.

Five ways to play:
- Known Lines: 10 hand-made routes with a finish and three stars each.
- Freeride: the endless mountain. How far can you go?
- Time Rush: 20 seconds on the clock, grab time bubbles to keep going.
- Avalanche: the snow is coming down behind you, crystal rings hold it back.
- Daily Line: a new route every day, the same for everyone.

Fish are your money. Spend them at the Fish Market on 18 creatures (penguins, a seal, a walrus, a narwhal, an orca, a snowy owl, a snow leopard and more). Each one has a power of its own and a comic of its own.

Ride through 14 stretches of mountain, each with its own weather and its own music: pine forest, glacier, crystal caves, cosmic ice, a frozen jungle and a neon sunset. Collect 33 trophies and finish three new tasks every day.
```

**Controls**

```
Tap, click, Space or Arrow Up: turn the other way.
Pause: the button in the bottom-right corner, a two-finger tap, P or Esc.
```

**Category**

```
Action
```

The form's list has no Casual. Not Clicker: on CrazyGames that means idle
games where clicking is the whole point, and the players it brings are not
looking for a game of timing.

**Tags** (at most 5, from the form's own list)

```
Skill, Running, Animal, Avoid, Top-Down
```

Next best, if one has to go: Obstacle, Collect, Speed.

**Settings in the form**

- Mobile: yes. The game fills any screen shape and plays with touch.
- Orientation: both. Portrait and landscape both fill the screen, with no letterbox.
- Languages: English (default), Russian, Romanian, Spanish, Portuguese, German.
- Content: PEGI 3 level. No violence: a crash only ends the run.
