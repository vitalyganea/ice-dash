How to play

  • The penguin slides on his belly, and always drifts to one side
  • Tap anywhere (or press Space) to slide the other way
  • That is the only control. Time your taps to weave between the pines and rocks
  • 🐟 Swallow fish as you pass — 25 points each
  • ✨ A golden fish is worth 150, but it never sits on the easy line
  • 🫧 A snow bubble takes one crash for you — grab it when you can
  • 🚩 Slip between the flag gates for a bonus
  • 🧊 On the glacier he turns lazily — tap earlier
  • 🌙 At night you see less of the hill ahead
  • One crash ends the run. How far can you get?
