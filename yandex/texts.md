## English (tab «Английский»)

**Name** — 23/50

```
Ice Dash: Penguin Slide
```

**SEO description** — 130/160

```
A penguin slides down an icy chute: one tap sends him the other way. Dodge pines and boulders, catch fish and unlock 18 creatures!
```

**About the game** — 927/1000

```
A penguin slides on his belly down an endless ice chute. He is always drifting to one side, and one tap sends him the other way. That is the whole control. Easy to learn, hard to master.

Five ways to play:
- Known Lines: 10 hand-made routes with a finish and three stars each.
- Freeride: the endless mountain. How far can you go?
- Time Rush: 20 seconds on the clock, grab time bubbles to keep going.
- Avalanche: the snow is coming down behind you, crystal rings hold it back.
- Daily Line: a new route every day, the same for everyone.

Fish are your money. The Fish Market has 18 creatures: penguins, a seal, a walrus, a narwhal, an orca, a snowy owl, a snow leopard and more. Each one has a power of its own and a comic of its own.

14 stretches of mountain, each with its own weather and its own music: pine forest, glacier, night run, crystal caves, cosmic ice, a neon sunset. 33 trophies and three new tasks every day.
```

**Short description** — 56/70

```
One tap sends the penguin the other way. Catch the fish!
```

**How to play** — 807/1000

```
Tap the screen, click, or press Space or Arrow Up: the penguin turns the other way. That is all there is to press.

Pines, boulders and crevasses leave one opening in every row. Time your tap to slip through it. Cross a crevasse by jumping off the ice ramp.

Catch fish: they are your money at the Fish Market. Golden fish are worth more. A snow bubble saves you from one crash. Slide through rings of ice crystals for bonus points. A ball of packed powder makes you unstoppable for six seconds.

Pass close to an obstacle for a close-call bonus. Fish in a row build a combo.

One crash ends the run unless you have a spare life. Spare lives are sold at the market.

Pause with the button in the bottom-right corner, a two-finger tap, or P or Esc.

New players start with a short tutorial right on the hill.
```
